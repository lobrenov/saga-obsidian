# Calendar view (as built)

A Bases view (`saga-calendar`) that shows the base's notes dated on one calendar note, as a Calendar (month or year grid), a Feed or a Timeline. A calendar is a note (`type: calendar`) holding `months`, `weekdays`, `first-weekday` and `eras`; a dated note links it under `calendar` and carries flat date keys (`year`, `month`, `day` or `era`, and `end-*`). The calendar note is never edited by date changes.

## Modules (`src/calendar`)
- `index.ts`: `registerCalendarView(plugin, config)` registers the hover source `saga-calendar` ("Saga calendar", `defaultMod: true`) and the view `saga-calendar` ("Saga calendar", icon `calendar-days`). Imports `calendar.css`.
- `options.ts`: `calendarOptions`: `calendar` (a `file` option whose filter accepts notes the config resolves to the `calendar` renderer), `display` (`calendar`, `feed`, `timeline`; default `calendar`), then the chosen display's options only: `opens` (`month`, `year`) for Calendar; `feed-order` (`oldest`, `newest`) and `banners` (toggle, default on) for Feed; `scale` (`all`, `century`, `decade`, `year`) for Timeline.
- `CalendarBasesView.ts`: the `BasesView`. Mounts React in a `saga-calview-view` div, renders on `onDataUpdated` and on config changes, caches the host while the calendar path is unchanged, unmounts and removes the div on unload. It carries `hoverPopover` and builds the `HostUi` (menu, hover, Edit dialog, Delete).
- `calendarState.ts`: `calendarStateOf(app, config, input)` turns the options and the base's files into a `CalendarState` (`types.ts`): `empty` with a message and issues (no calendar chosen, not in the vault, not a calendar note, not a valid calendar; an issue of an unset key reads "Not set"), or `calendar` (host, file, title, parsed calendar, entries, display, `opens`, `scale`, `order`, `banners`, `setOption`). The `calendar` option may hold a vault path, a `[[link]]` or a bare name. `isCalendarNote`.
- `entries.ts`: `collectEntries` builds the `CalendarEntry` list. `entryDate.ts`: `dateText`. `opening.ts`: the year and month a display opens on (the earliest dated entry's, else month 1 of year 1).
- `host.ts`: `CalendarHost` (`openNote`, `excerpt`, `hoverNote`, `showMenu`, `editEntry`, `deleteEntry`) and `createHost(app, calendar, ui)`; `HostUi` is the part that opens Obsidian UI.
- `CalendarRoot.tsx`: the empty state (the note's name as a link, the issues) or the chosen display, and the entry menu. `MonthGrid.tsx`, `YearGrid.tsx`, `Chips.tsx`, `placement.ts` (`place`), `bars.ts` (`weekBars`): the Calendar display. `FeedDisplay.tsx`, `feed.ts` (`buildFeed`): the Feed. `TimelineDisplay.tsx`, `timelineAxis.ts` (`axisFor`, `markOf`, `position`, `titleOf`, windows), `timelineRows.ts`: the Timeline.
- `EntryCard.tsx`, `NoteLink.tsx`, `Glyph.tsx` (`setIcon`), `useExcerpt.ts`: shared by the displays.
- `EditEntry.tsx`, `editModal.ts`, `entryActions.ts` (`entryNoun`, `saveEntry`, `deleteEntry`): Edit and Delete.
- `src/shared/calendars/schema.ts`: `calendarSchema`/`parseCalendar`, `dateKeysSchema`. `dates.ts`: `readDate`, `checkDate`, `compareDates`, `formatDate`, `eraOf`, `eraSpan`, `dateRange`, `dayNumber`/`fromDayNumber`, `weekdayOf`, `monthGrid`, `addMonths`, `addYears`, `dateKeys`, `shortNames`. `src/header/noteCalendar.ts`: `noteCalendar`, `loreCalendarKey`, `NO_CALENDAR`.

## Data
- Calendar note: `months` (`{ name, days }`, at least one), `weekdays` (unique names, at least one), `first-weekday` (one of the weekdays, the weekday of 1 <first month> 1), `eras` (`{ name, short, side: before | after, start? }`, earliest first, `before` eras first, unique names). The first era of each side has no `start` (dropped if given): the earliest `before` era runs back without end, the first `after` era starts at year 1. Later `start`s count from 1 away from year 1, falling for `before` eras and rising for `after` eras.
- Dated note: `year`, `month`, `day` (integers; no year 0, 1-based month and day; a finer key needs the coarser), or `era` alone (an era name, with no year). The end is the same keys under `end-`. Precision is the finest key present. Year -1 precedes year 1.
- Day numbers count from 1 <first month> 1 (`dayNumber`). `dateRange` gives a date's first and last day at its precision; an era covers its span, infinite where open. `compareDates` sorts by year, month, day, a coarser date before a finer one at the same point and an era-only date at its era's start. `formatDate`: `28 Highsun 1312 AL`, `Highsun 1312 AL`, `1312 AL` (the era's `short`, the year unsigned), or the era's name.

## Entries
- `collectEntries(app, config, files, calendar file, calendar)`: for each markdown file of the base (not the calendar note) keep those whose link under the Lore renderer's `calendar` key (`loreCalendarKey`) resolves, from the note, to the calendar note. Any renderer qualifies.
- Start and end are read with `readDate` and checked with `checkDate` (unknown era, month beyond the calendar's, day beyond its month's); an invalid start is `undefined`, an invalid end or one before the start is dropped, each with an issue. A note with no start and no issue is left out; an end without a start is the issue "Ends without When".
- Each entry carries its file, title (basename), renderer, `type` as written, start, end, issues and banner (`bannerOf` resolved to a resource URL and `y`).

## Displays
Each display has a header (title, "on <calendar>" as a `NoteLink`, navigation, a toggle group writing its view option) and a content area. Clicking an entry opens the `EntryCard`; Esc or a click elsewhere closes it. Entries without a valid start are "Date problem" chips.
- Calendar: Month (`MonthGrid`, weeks of the calendar's weekdays with neighbouring days, weekday names shortened by `shortNames` in narrow views) or Year (`YearGrid`, a small grid per month). Previous/Next and PageUp/PageDown step a month or a year; the toggle writes `opens`. Day-dated entries are bars, one per week across the days they cover, in lanes (`weekBars`); above 3 lanes a "+N" button lists the rest in a menu. In Year, a day with entries is a button (a menu when several; faint when no entry starts that day). Month- and year-dated entries in range are "No day set" chips. Era-only entries are not shown.
- Feed: entries along a central spine, alternating sides, sorted by `compareDates` (ties keep the base's order); the toggle writes `feed-order`. An era header opens each run in one era, a gap marker gives whole years between entries more than a year apart (from the latest end so far), a year marker opens a run sharing a year and the dates under it leave the year out. Each card shows the banner (96 px, `banner-y`; when `banners` is on), the date with a precision tag below day, the title as a link and an excerpt read as the card nears the viewport. The node marks day, month or year, and era precision. Below 760 px the spine moves to the left edge.
- Timeline: one row per entry (label button, track with its mark and date): a point for a day, a bar to its end, a span for a month, year or era. Scales: All (one column per era, weighted in years; an open era is drawn `OPEN_ERA_YEARS` (300) or as far as entries reach; one column without eras), Century and Decade (a column per year), Year (a column per month). Below All, Previous/Next and PageUp/PageDown step a window, and an overview strip shows the eras, a tick per entry and the window. Marks past the window's edges are open. Only entries meeting the window are listed.
- Card (`EntryCard`): renderer, the title as a link, `dateText` (weekday first for a single day), a 140-character excerpt and issues; beside its anchor (`data-slot`) on the side with room, below it full width under 560 px. Focus moves in and returns on close.
- Styling: `calendar.css` (`saga-calview-*`, `saga-entry-edit-*`), `feed.css` (`saga-feed-*`), `timeline.css` (`saga-timeline-*`). `data-renderer` sets `--saga-calview-accent` and `--saga-calview-tint` from `--saga-<renderer>` and `--saga-<renderer>-tint`. Layout variables are set from code (`--saga-calview-columns`, `--saga-calview-card-*`, `--saga-feed-banner-y`, `--saga-timeline-left`, `-width`, `-weight`, `-columns`). Coarse pointers get larger targets.

## Edit and Delete
- A right-click, the ContextMenu key or a touch held `HOLD_MS` within `HOLD_SLOP_PX` on an element with `data-entry` opens a menu: "Edit <noun>…" and "Delete <noun>". The noun is `entryNoun`: the note's `type` (lowercased unless all capitals), else the renderer's label. The click ending a held touch does not open the card.
- Edit (`EditEntryModal`, titled "Edit <noun>"): Title, When and Ends (`DateEditor` from the header, on `noteCalendar`'s calendar). Update or Mod+Enter calls `saveEntry`: refuses an empty title, one with `\ / : * " < > ? |`, one clashing (case-insensitively) with another note, or Ends before When, showing the reason; renames with `fileManager.renameFile` when the title changed, then writes the changed dates in one `setFields`, removing finer and era keys (`dateKeys`). Esc, the close button or a click outside closes an open date picker first, else discards the draft.
- Delete: a confirm (`Delete <noun>`, `Delete “<name>”?`), then `fileManager.trashFile`. A failure raises a Notice.

## What Obsidian provides
- `Menu` (`showMenu`) for the entry menu, "+N" and Year-day lists; `Modal` for Edit and the Delete confirm (`confirm` in `src/map/modals.ts`).
- Open note: `NoteLink` calls `workspace.openLinkText(path, calendarPath, false)`; ⌘/Ctrl-click uses `'tab'`.
- Hover preview: `workspace.trigger('hover-link', { event, source: 'saga-calendar', hoverParent: view, targetEl, linktext, sourcePath })` on pointer-over of a `NoteLink`.
- `fileManager.renameFile` and `fileManager.trashFile` (Obsidian's Deleted files setting); `vault.cachedRead` for excerpts; `setIcon` for glyphs.
